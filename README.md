# 🌿 LetterForMyself — Mindful Journaling & Mental Wellness Companion

A tactile, local-first mental health and journaling companion designed with a **Warm Botanical & Tactile Paper** aesthetic (cozy cream, sage green, terracotta clay, and realistic notebook textures). Built with full bilingual support (**English** & **Bahasa Indonesia**).

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🪶 1. Tactile Journaling Studio
- **6 Realistic Paper Textures**: *Warm Parchment*, *Botanical Ruled*, *Architect Dot-Grid*, *Pressed Herbarium*, *Terracotta Blush*, and *Midnight Starlight (Dark Mode)*.
- **3 Ink Typography Styles**: *Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`)*, *Clean Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)*, and *Handwritten Ink (`Caveat`)*.
- **14 Botanical & Mood Stickers**: Stamp decorative pressed-flora and mood emblems (`🌿`, `🍵`, `🕯️`, `🌻`, `🦋`, `❤️‍🩹`, `🌙`, `💌`, etc.) onto the corner of your notebook pages.
- **Voice-to-Text Dictation**: Speak freely in English (`en-US`) or Bahasa Indonesia (`id-ID`) to transcribe thoughts directly onto your page.
- **Built-in Ambient Soundscape Bar**: Toggle *Gentle Rain on Leaves*, *Woodland Breeze*, *Cozy Hearth Fire*, or a *432Hz Singing Bowl* directly inside the writing studio.

### 🌱 2. Guided CBT & Psychological Templates
- **CBT Thought Reframer**: Interactive 5-step Cognitive Behavioral Therapy flow:
  1. Triggering Situation
  2. Automatic Negative Thought & Initial Belief Slider (`0–100%`)
  3. Interactive **Cognitive Distortions Identifier** (*All-or-Nothing*, *Catastrophizing*, *Mind Reading*, *Emotional Reasoning*, *"Should" Statements*, etc.) with gentle reframing tips
  4. Evidence For vs. Evidence Supporting a Kinder Truth
  5. Balanced Compassionate Reframe & Relief Shift percentage
- **Anxiety Unload & Circle of Control Matrix**: Separate what is *Within Your Control* from what you have *Permission to Release*, plus one gentle next step.
- **7 Ritual Templates**: *Freeflow Sanctuary*, *CBT Thought Reframer*, *Anxiety Unload*, *Morning Intention Ritual*, *Evening Wind-Down*, *Self-Compassion Pause*, and *Time-Capsule Letter*.

### 📅 3. Interactive Calendar, Somatic Body Map & Insights
- **Monthly Emotional & Writing Calendar**: Inspect daily mood weather and journal pages written on any date.
- **Somatic Body Sensation Tracker**: Map where emotions live physically (*Deep steady breath*, *Warm chest*, *Soft jaw*, *Heavy shoulders*, *Tight chest*, etc.).
- **CBT Reframe Analytics**: Track which cognitive distortions you untangle most frequently and your average distress reduction.
- **Mindful Herbarium Companion**: Grow your virtual botanical plant from a *Quiet Seed* (`🌰`) to a *Blooming Camellia* (`🌸`) as you journal and practice breathwork.

### 🌬️ 4. Breathwork, Soundscape Mixer & 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
- **Guided Breath Pacer**: *Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)*, *4-7-8 Tranquil Breath*, and *Vagus Nerve Soother (4-6)*.
- **Web Audio Ambient Mixer**: Offline multi-channel procedural synthesizer for rain, pine wind, crackling hearth, and harmonic singing bowls.
- **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding SOS**: Step-by-step interactive panic & overwhelm anchor.
- **100% Local-First Privacy**: Optional 4-digit PIN lock and 1-click JSON backup export/import.
